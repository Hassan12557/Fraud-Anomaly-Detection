

\# Data Quality Assessment — ROCCC



\## Reliable

The dataset is associated with research from the

Worldline and ULB Machine Learning Group.

However, the limited collection period restricts

how confidently results generalize.



Assessment: Moderate to good.



\## Original

The dataset contains real transaction records,

rather than artificially generated transactions.



Assessment: Good.



\## Comprehensive

The dataset has 28 anonymized PCA components,

Time, Amount, and Class. Important business

context such as merchant and device information

is unavailable.



Assessment: Limited.



\## Current

The transactions were collected in September 2013.

Fraud patterns and payment technologies may have

changed substantially since then.



Assessment: Weak.



\## Cited

The dataset has a traceable Kaggle distribution page

and identifiable research provenance.



Assessment: Good.



\## Overall Conclusion

Suitable for learning, experimentation, and

benchmarking anomaly-detection methods.



Not sufficient on its own to establish

production readiness or current banking performance.
